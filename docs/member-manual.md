# Skill-Swap member manual

## Starting an exchange

Create an account with your name, email, and password. The Gmail SMTP verification code confirms the email before the account can become active. Complete the onboarding steps with a display name, avatar, biography, skills you can teach, skills you want to learn, timezone, availability, privacy choices, and recording preference.

## Becoming a Verified Teacher

Open **My skills**, choose a teaching skill, read the assessment instructions, and complete the randomized 30–40 question assessment. Results are calculated by the server. A passed attempt grants a Verified Teacher status only for that specific skill. Failed attempts follow the configured retake cooldown.

## Finding a partner

Open **Discover matches** to see reciprocal suggestions. Each suggestion explains the learning skill you want, the verified teaching skill the other member offers, and shared availability. Send a request only when the exchange feels appropriate. Chat becomes available after the other member accepts.

## Chat and sessions

Use the private conversation to introduce yourself, compare goals, and schedule a session. A session includes the date, timezone, agenda, participants, and recording-consent state. The ZegoCloud room opens only for the two authorized participants and receives short-lived credentials from Django.

## Recording and certificates

Recording requires explicit consent and shows a visible recording indicator. Recording references are private and use signed Cloudinary access. After the session ends, both participants confirm completion. If no dispute or safety hold exists, Skill-Swap creates one certificate per eligible completed exchange and provides a public validation link.

## Safety

Use report and block controls when a profile, message, or session feels unsafe. Do not share sensitive personal information in chat. Admins monitor platform activity metadata and investigate private content only under an authorized, reasoned, audited safety workflow.
